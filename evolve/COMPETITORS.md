## Where NightCast already leads
- No autoplay-next and no queue: the single biggest bedtime complaint across Apple, Spotify and Pocket Casts (voice-of-customer Q1-Q10).
- One-tap Moon on the main player and a ~6 minute fade; rivals fade for seconds and users ask for longer (Q24-Q30).
- No account, no ads, no analytics.

## Where NightCast trails
1. Still awake when Sleep ends: no hands-free way to turn it back on (Castro, Overcast, Pocket Casts, Sleepcast all have one).
2. No "where did I drift off" (only Sleepcast answers it).
3. Sleep is invisible outside the app (Castro / Pocket Casts show it on the Lock Screen).
4. The Moon is only on Now Playing; the mini player has no Moon.
5. No automatic Sleep at night (Pocket Casts request, Sleepcast Time Window).
6. No loudness levelling for loud ads and outros (Overcast Voice Boost).
7. No Siri / Shortcuts / widgets.
