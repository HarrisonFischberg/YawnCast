YawnCast is a podcast player for falling asleep. No account or sign-in is needed. Search for any public podcast (search uses Apple's iTunes Search API) or pick one in Explore, open an episode, press Play, then tap the Moon to turn on Sleep. Sleep fades the volume down over about six minutes and pauses. Audio streams directly from each podcast's publisher; the app has no servers, ads, or analytics. Background audio is used only to keep the podcast playing while the phone is locked.
