YawnCast is a podcast player made for falling asleep.

Put on a show, tap the Moon, and close your eyes. A while later the volume starts stepping down, a little at a time, until the episode quietly pauses. Your place is saved, so tomorrow night you can pick up right where you drifted off.

SLEEP, IN ONE TAP
• Tap the Moon to turn Sleep on. The fade starts later and takes about six minutes, slow enough that it won't wake you.
• Want more time, or less? Nudge the fade by five minutes either way.
• YawnCast never starts the next episode on its own. When it stops, it stays stopped.
• Late at night, Sleep switches itself on, and the Lock Screen shows when the fade begins.
• Wake up in the night? One tap takes you back to where you drifted off.

YOUR PODCASTS, CALMLY
• New: the latest episodes from your shows, by day.
• Library: your shows as a grid or a list, in the order you choose.
• Explore: shows picked from your interests and Apple's top charts.
• One search bar finds your shows and new ones as you type.
• A small player follows you on every screen.

MADE FOR THE DARK
• Ten themes: six animated night Scenes (Midnight, Mountain Vista, Beach, Rainforest, Underwater, City Scape) and four calm colors (Paper, Ember, Ink, Red Light).
• Red Light keeps blue light out of the screen entirely.
• Dark by default, with big, easy-to-hit buttons for sleepy thumbs.

NOTHING TO SIGN UP FOR
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Your library lives on your iPhone. Shows come straight from their publishers, and search uses Apple's public podcast directory.

Sweet dreams.
